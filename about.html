About me.

From mathematical questions to working systems.

My work brings together mathematical research and software development for robotics and automation. My background spans algorithm design, simulation, and embedded systems, with experience in C++, Python, and CUDA.

At Durham University, my research focuses on multi-agent path finding and algorithmic graph theory. In industry, I work as a mathematician at VersaTile Automation and collaborate with Tharsus.
